# Retail Sales and Profitability EDA

## Project Overview

This project presents an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of retail sales and profitability using Tableau. The analysis uses Tableau's Sample – Superstore dataset to explore sales performance, profitability, trends, customer segments, product categories, regional performance, and potential outliers.

## Objectives

The main objectives of the analysis are to:

- Examine overall sales and profit performance
- Identify monthly sales and profit trends
- Compare profitability across product categories and sub-categories
- Analyze regional and state-level profitability
- Examine customer segment performance
- Explore the relationship between discount levels and profit
- Identify potential sales and profit outliers

## Dataset

The analysis uses Tableau's Sample – Superstore dataset.

The dataset contains retail order information including:

- Order and shipping dates
- Customer and segment information
- Geographic information
- Product categories and sub-categories
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit

Source: Tableau Sample Data  
https://public.tableau.com/app/learn/sample-data

## Tools Used

- Tableau Desktop
- Microsoft Word
- GitHub

## Key Findings

- Total sales were approximately **2.33 million**.
- Total profit was approximately **292,297**.
- Technology generated the highest category-level profit.
- The West region generated the highest regional profit.
- Consumer was the highest-profit customer segment.
- Profitability varied substantially across product sub-categories.
- The analysis identified both positive and negative-profit observations requiring further investigation.
- The relationship between discount and profit varied by product category and should be interpreted as an association rather than a causal relationship.

## Conclusion

The exploratory analysis provides an overview of the company's sales and profitability patterns and highlights areas that could be investigated further. The findings can support deeper analysis of product performance, discounting practices, geographic profitability, and unusual transactions.
